# R085 Nested Hierarchical Gaussian 0-10k Gate

Status date: 2026-09-02.

Status: nested representation and Panda count diagnostic complete; `256` is
selected as the level-1 trainer candidate. No trainer, checkpoint-schema,
optimizer, launcher, HGC, or formal training integration is yet claimed. R080
remains the matched learned 4k evidence source. R084 remains a closed
topology-RBF diagnostic route and is not reopened by R085.

## Objective

R085 asks whether a nested two-resolution Gaussian attribute field can let fur
learn broad, coherent structure before local residuals become trainable. The
gate is limited to iterations `0-10000`. It does not authorize a 20k fine
attribute field or a full 30k run.

The representation must remain species-, image-region-, and view-independent.
Panda is the first training sample; white tiger supplies matched initialization
and regression evidence before any method claim.

## Population Hierarchy

The existing `4500` primary guide roots remain the level-2 sites and retain
their exact clean-flow face IDs, barycentric coordinates, fixed XYZ, target
directions, confidence, and root ordering. They are loaded from the clean-flow
target rather than regenerated by the trainer.

Level 1 is a strict subset of those 4500 sites. One deterministic,
topology-aware, density-preserving FPS order is built once; its prefixes of
`256`, `512`, and `1024` are the predeclared diagnostic candidates. The
existing guide ordering must not be treated as an FPS prefix: the accepted
guide set contains separately constructed head/body sampling and may be
reordered by its source artifact.

Level 3 is disabled. The historical `20000` secondary-geometry residual roots
are not R085 level 3. In the active route `CHILD_COUNT=1`, so historical
`child_radius`/child-spread state has no strand-expansion forward effect and is
excluded from R085 attribute ownership.

## Nested Gaussian Evaluation

Level 1 is evaluated only at the 4500 level-2 sites:

```text
A_4500 = G_1->2(C_1) + s_2(t) R_4500.
```

The resulting level-2 coefficients are evaluated once at render roots:

```text
A_render = G_2->render(A_4500).
```

Both operators use nonnegative row-normalized compact C2 Gaussian weights.
Consequently constants survive both stages. Level 1 must never create another
direct 400k-root traversal. Its expected sparse work is proportional to only
`4500 * local_support`, not the render-root population.

Coarse candidates must be topology-valid before Gaussian weights are
evaluated. A large ambient-space ellipsoid may not couple surface-distant
sheets merely because they are close in XYZ. Ordinary optimization keeps both
levels' XYZ fixed. Any later population change is an explicit lifecycle event
and requires rebinding.

Strictly positive properties compose in one log/asinh-ratio latent and decode
once. Bounded ratios compose in their existing unconstrained/logit latent.
Direction retains the absolute 4500 clean-flow field and uses transported
tangent residuals; independently normalized direction levels must not be
added together.

## 0-10k Gradient Contract

The level-2 operator is present in the forward path from iteration zero. A
frozen level does not mean that its propagation is bypassed.

- `0-3000`: level-1 trainable attributes may receive image gradients. The
  level-2 residual coefficients and level-2 covariance parameters are frozen
  at zero/reference state. Gradients pass through the fixed level-2 operator
  into level 1.
- `3000-4000`: the level-2 residual contribution ramps continuously from zero;
  there is no step-3001 forward jump.
- `4000-10000`: the selected level-2 attributes remain active. The exact
  covariance-unlock boundary is not authorized until the initialization and
  execution diagnostics pass.
- Level 3 remains absent through 10k.

Freezing should remove parameters from active autograd/optimizer work where
possible, rather than computing gradients and clearing them after backward.
It must not detach the level-1-to-render value path.

## Attribute Ownership

- **Length:** uniform data-derived global reference, level-1 spatial field,
  then zero-initialized level-2 log-ratio residual after 3k.
- **Root width, tip ratio, taper:** global or level 1 only in R085; no level-2
  residual without width-specific evidence.
- **Directed flow:** the fixed absolute 4500 clean-flow direction participates
  in every forward pass. Its level-2 trainable tangent residual is frozen
  through 3k and may ramp later; the base direction is never downsampled to
  level 1.
- **Color/DC:** may use level 2 when the representation is integrated; no new
  high-order SH or 20k appearance level is part of this gate.
- **Curl/shape:** no new fine ownership before 10k.
- **Child spread:** excluded. `CHILD_COUNT=1` makes child expansion an exact
  early return in the active forward path.

Every channel is packed into one traversal for each active Gaussian level.
An attribute that stops at level 1 does not receive a level-2 residual tensor.

## Pre-Training Count Diagnostic

The `256/512/1024` candidates are compared without training on the same Panda
checkpoint geometry and one shared visualization protocol. Required evidence:

1. strict nested subset identity and deterministic repeated bytes;
2. complete topology-valid level-1-to-level-2 coverage with no fallback;
3. finite nonnegative row-normalized weights and constant reproduction;
4. candidate, active, effective-neighbor, and maximum-weight distributions;
5. fixed view-09 root-location and influence/coverage maps;
6. one shared-range length stress map formed from the same learned 4k guide
   values, explicitly labeled as a bandwidth diagnostic rather than an R085
   initialization or training result;
7. runtime, memory, source hashes, report, and manifest.

The selected count is the smallest candidate that retains complete smooth
coverage without isolated dominant weights or visually implausible loss of
large anatomical transitions. Count symmetry or powers of two are not
acceptance evidence.

## Directed-Flow Color Maps

R085 adds one reusable canonical direction-color protocol. The visible 3D
direction is projected into the selected camera; screen angle is encoded as a
cyclic HSV hue with `right -> down -> left -> up -> right`. Saturation and
value remain one, so color records direction rather than projected magnitude.
Near-zero projected vectors are neutral gray. The underlying direction is
always parallel-transported before Gaussian blending and normalized only after
the weighted vector sum.

The first implementation stamped visible render roots onto rounded pixels with
hard radius-2 disks and last-writer replacement. It is retained only as a
root-sampling diagnostic: its pixel texture is not field-continuity evidence.
The canonical smooth-field view instead evaluates the same Gaussian direction
at all `340288` mesh vertices, interpolates unit screen vectors over `680572`
shared triangles, converts the per-pixel vector to hue, and covers `429891`
visible surface pixels. It uses no point stamping, screen-space blur, or base
texture inside the surface mask.

The initial surface-raster attempt correctly refused two vertices whose
area-weighted accumulated normal was zero. Exact mesh inspection found both to
be orphan vertices that rasterize no triangle. The deterministic repair assigns
the first valid incident face normal to cancellation rows and a canonical
placeholder only to true orphans; the two orphans never contribute a visible
pixel. The failed runner record and staging directory are preserved. Retry 1
validated the repair with zero degenerate visible direction pixels; retry 2
sets surface alpha to one so no fur texture is mistaken for field variation.

The fixed 4500 clean-flow base is visually continuous in view 09. Its
pre-normalization magnitude P05/P50 is `0.956/0.997`, with no negative or
greater-than-90-degree comparison to itself. The learned R080 4k direction
changes from that base by median/P95/P99 `6.66/18.88/26.20` degrees, with zero
negative directions.

Downsampling the **absolute** clean-flow field is intentionally a stress test,
not the R085 forward contract:

This evidence does not change the scalar-count selection. It confirms the

## Primary-Guide Density Attribution

The complete 4500-guide audit rejects the simple explanation that the head is
globally much denser than the body. Euclidean `d8`
min/P05/P50/P95/max is
`0.02876/0.03791/0.04398/0.04953/0.05690`. The historical 500-root group has
mean `0.03998`; the 4000-root group has mean `0.04441`, only about ten percent
larger. The population is visually well distributed in aggregate.

Two different defects remain:

1. Per-guide Euclidean and topology `d8` form spatial blue-noise patterns even
   though their histograms are narrow. Using raw local FPS spacing directly as
   every Gaussian reference scale therefore injects a noisy bandwidth field
   before any attribute is learned.
2. The accepted roots were sampled as separate historical 500/4000 groups and
   merged. Every one of the 39 guides whose nearest distance is below half the
   global median has its nearest neighbor in the other historical group.
   Likewise all 42 guides with `d8/d1 > 3` cross that boundary. Their nearest
   transported clean-flow direction difference has median/P95/max
   `1.64/14.24/37.02` degrees. The closest pair is only `0.00323` apart versus
   global median `d1=0.02261`.

This is evidence against adding attribute-specific bandwidths as the first
repair. The next fixed-checkpoint gate keeps one shared Gaussian weight field
for all attributes and compares representation-wide choices only: current
4500 roots with one uniform median reference radius, the same roots with one
uniformly enlarged radius, and a smaller unified nested-FPS population. The
historical region labels remain diagnostic evidence and are not an algorithmic
rule.

## Shared-Field Count And Bandwidth Gate

The next Panda fixed-checkpoint gate compares one shared field across every
attribute. It predeclares current adaptive-`d8` 4500, uniform-median 4500,
uniform-`1.25x` 4500, nested topology-FPS 2048 with adaptive or uniform `d8`,
and a sixth follow-up whose 4500 radii are the M256 nested-Gaussian
reconstruction of `log(d8)`.

No arm creates a direction-specific field, reversal, or greater-than-90-degree
Panda direction. The fixed Panda evidence is:

| arm | vertex candidate pairs | direction change P50 / P95 | angular-gradient >10 / >20 px fraction | learned-length max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| adaptive 4500 | 6.706M | 0 / 0.03 deg | 0.362% / 0.134% | 0.08416 |
| uniform 4500 | 6.714M | 0.22 / 1.38 deg | 0.358% / 0.132% | 0.08404 |
| uniform 1.25x 4500 | 10.632M | 0.51 / 2.84 deg | 0.358% / 0.131% | 0.07290 |
| smooth-M256 bandwidth, 4500 | 6.665M | 0.21 / 1.31 deg | 0.361% / 0.133% | 0.08346 |
| adaptive 2048 | 6.510M | 1.33 / 7.74 deg | 0.369% / 0.135% | 0.07548 |
| uniform 2048 | 6.444M | 1.35 / 7.77 deg | 0.361% / 0.140% | 0.07794 |

Uniform or smoothly reconstructed bandwidth removes the blue-noise scale map,
but neither reduces the rapid direction transition. Uniform `1.25x` adds about
58% candidate work, and 2048 changes direction more strongly, without an
angular-gradient benefit. Therefore count and bandwidth are not the primary
cause of that transition; it is already encoded in the clean-flow direction
values.

The matched white-tiger gate rejects both apparently safe Panda bandwidth
repairs. White tiger has adaptive pre-normalization magnitude as low as
`0.00296`, exposing ambient-space support cancellation. Uniform-median 4500
introduces `74` negative directions, including `37` above 120 degrees. The
smooth-M256 bandwidth improves this only to `65/36`; it still fails. This is
not evidence for retaining noisy `d8`. It proves that the current Euclidean
candidate pattern is not a safe foundation for bandwidth changes.

No shared-field arm advances to training. R085 must next implement a
topology-gated guide-to-query candidate pattern shared by every attribute,
then repeat the same bandwidth gate. Candidate topology is a representation
repair, not a direction-specific exception.

## Stop Conditions

R085 stops before trainer integration if any candidate requires a
species/region/view rule, ambient cross-sheet fallback, negative weights,
post-output smoothing, uncovered rows, or a nonzero initialization residual.

After integration, any new coherent bald island, isolated length/coverage
blob, cell boundary, flow reversal/foldback, or held-out-view shimmer is a
visual stop even when aggregate reconstruction loss improves.
